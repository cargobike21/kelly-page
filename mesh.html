Kelly Page for MeshCore

Put other hams' MeshCore nodes on your radio in one go, and list yours so they can add you.

Open Ham Contacts

Kelly Page runs in your browser. It talks to your MeshCore node over USB (Web Serial) or Bluetooth, so nothing needs installing.

Open Kelly › LoRa Mesh › Ham Contacts

Then: connect your node, turn on Use the P25X ham directory, and press Add all not yet on my radio.

On an iPhone or iPad

Safari on iOS cannot talk to a MeshCore node, so use the official MeshCore app and import contacts by QR code:

  • On a computer or Android phone, open Kelly › Ham Contacts, and press the QR button on a ham's row -- or make one below from a name and public key.
  • In the MeshCore app: Contacts › Add contact › Scan QR.

Make a contact QR

For the official MeshCore app: a node's name, public key and type become a meshcore://contact/add QR. Public keys only -- never type a private key.
