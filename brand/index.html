Brand guidelines

How to use the Kelly Page name, logo and colours. The mark is a working dog in profile, ear up, listening -- the amber signal arcs say what the product does.

01 · Logo suiteSix files

Use the horizontal lockup wherever there's room for the name. Use the app icon or emblem where space is square. Always use the supplied files; never redraw or retype the logo.

04 · TypographyClear, sturdy, uppercase

The name is set in a bold humanist sans, tracked slightly, in capitals. The tagline is semibold with wide tracking. For everything else, use the same family in sentence case.

05 · Do & don'tUse it well

DoUse the dark lockup on navy and dark photos.
DoUse the light lockup on white and print.
Don'tRecolour the mark. It is slate and amber.
Don'tStretch, squash or rotate the logo.
Don'tPlace logos on busy photos without a solid panel.
Don'tFlip the mark. It always faces forward, to the right.
Kellypage
Don'tRetype the name in other fonts or cases.
Don'tFade the logo or use it as a low-contrast pattern.
